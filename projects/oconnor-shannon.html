O'Connor Shannon is a boutique Dublin estate agency established in 1995, with deep expertise in the pre-'63 multi-unit investment market. I overhauled the brand and its online presence to underline the firm's standing in the Irish property market.

Research before design

I took time to understand how the firm began and developed, researched its target audiences, and mapped customer journeys and profiles. That understanding shaped both the updated brand and a marketing approach that engages existing clients while attracting new ones.

A progressive look online

The challenge was a clean, contemporary identity with a stronger digital pull: more enquiries, more sign-ups, more sales. I modernised the website with bespoke imagery, new photography and graphics, building a responsive, accessible platform with clearly mapped user journeys that positions O'Connor Shannon as a go-to agency.

Full-funnel support

The online presence launched with complete digital marketing support behind it, ensuring the refreshed brand reached both its established audience and a new generation of property clients.
