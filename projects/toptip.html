TopTip

Charity Gaming

Brand IdentityApp DesignUX/UI

TopTip is an interactive prediction game that lets sports fans play against each other while raising money for causes they care about, fun betting that helps charities. Working from an open brief, I created the brand and designed the app experience.

A trophy hidden in the name

The logo is a minimal, abstract trophy formed by two Ts: Top for the ambition of reaching the summit, Tip for the placing of predictions. Energetic, bold colours capture the spirit of sport around a clean, modern mark, deliberately warmer and friendlier than the cold, serious betting apps it stands apart from.

Designing the game

I studied the target users and the best of the popular sports and betting app patterns, then designed a friendly, intuitive interface for the game on iPhone and Android, plus a standalone landing site to give the project visibility and drive downloads.
