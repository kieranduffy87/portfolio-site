Liffey Meats

Born of the Land

Brand IdentityDigital DesignDigital Marketing

Liffey Meats is a fifth-generation, family-owned Irish beef processor and one of the country's largest beef exporters. Their identity no longer conveyed their scale, quality standards or sustainability commitments. I delivered a full brand refresh and a new digital platform that honours the heritage while projecting modern confidence.

Auditing the brand

I began with an audit of the existing brand assets and digital touchpoints, finding the opportunities to modernise without losing authenticity. A refreshed logo, streamlined typography and a nature-inspired palette created a more distinctive, contemporary expression rooted in the company's regional farming story.

A platform worthy of the story

The new website is fully responsive and UX-driven: rich imagery, intuitive navigation and clear messaging around quality, sustainability and heritage. It showcases global reach while celebrating the family-run roots, giving the brand storytelling power its old platform lacked.

Ready for export markets

As the company expanded into the Netherlands, I built an export positioning framework and market-ready brand expression tailored to European buyers, refining messaging around provenance, traceability and premium grass-fed quality. A cohesive launch toolkit lets the brand communicate confidently in new markets while staying authentically Irish.
