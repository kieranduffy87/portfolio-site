KCMG

Engineering Excellence

Brand IdentityDigital DesignMotion

KCMG is a leading mechanical building services engineering company delivering projects across Ireland and mainland Europe. The rebrand set out to modernise the identity while honouring a legacy of engineering excellence, and to elevate the entire brand experience, not just the look.

A bold new identity

I introduced a bold visual identity and completely redesigned the website to communicate KCMG's solutions clearly to both clients and investors. The upgrade went beyond aesthetics, streamlining messaging, enhancing usability and reinforcing credibility across every touchpoint, in close collaboration with stakeholders around the world.

Digitally driven, brand powered

The key challenge was translating core brand values into a cohesive digital narrative. I designed a client-centric web platform with intuitive UX, dynamic content and consistent visual storytelling, a refined digital ecosystem that strengthens the brand on every screen.

Detail-first UX

From wireframes and interactive prototypes through to build, the process was driven by user research. Every section was carefully structured and the full user journey mapped and refined, resulting in a responsive, agile site with a clean, modern interface that puts KCMG ahead of its competition.
