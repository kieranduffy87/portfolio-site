Over several years I've worked with the National Museum of Ireland, the Country Life Museum in Mayo, Carlow County Museum and the Local Authority Museums Network, supporting brand strategy, digital platforms and exhibitions across Ireland's museum community.

The Local Authority Museums Network

LAMN represents twelve local authority museums that play a vital role in the social, cultural and economic life of their regions, from Carlow and Cavan to Galway, Kerry and Waterford. I designed an engaging, dashboard-style website for the network, giving its rich collections and imaginative programmes a shared digital home.

Carlow County Museum

I supported Carlow County Museum, a designated museum under the National Monuments Acts and part of the Heritage Council's Museum Standards Programme, with its brand strategy and digital platform, helping the museum reach and engage new audiences.

Exhibitions and print

The work extends across digital and print media, including support for touring exhibitions such as Whitewash and Thatch, a fascinating collection of 1930s–40s architectural drawings of Irish thatched cottages shown at the Country Life museum and regional venues. Every design is simple to interact with and friendly on any device.
